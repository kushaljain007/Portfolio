Cardiff University · 2025–Present

Laser Frequency Stabilization & High-power Interferometer Commissioning

Team Scope

Collaborative QUEST programme team (~5–6 researchers: 2 professors, 2 postdoctoral researchers, and 2 PhD researchers). Individual contributions focused on optical commissioning, lock optimisation, and transfer-function-driven diagnostics.

System

Co-located power-recycled Michelson interferometers (using 1064 nm Nd:YAG lasers and neoLASE GmbH OEM solid-state laser amplifier systems, and active control loops) for high-sensitivity precision measurement experiments.

Issue

Mechanical coupling and loop instabilities limited lock robustness and noise performance. A second major focus was increasing laser power build-up in the arm cavity to improve SNR and reduce relative shot-noise impact.

Action
  • Implemented PDH locking using both dither-lock and DC-readout locking methods
  • Measured and tuned loop behaviour using DARM transfer-function measurement
  • Optimised alignment to improve power build-up and operational repeatability
Technical Intervention — Noise Mitigation Through Passive Vibration Isolation
  • Led passive vibration isolation implementation using Viton damping pads; postdoctoral support from Dr Fabio Bergamin during selected runs
  • Diagnosed mechanical coupling pathways using DARM transfer-function measurement
  • Achieved ~10× RMS noise reduction following installation
  • Improved lock robustness and operational stability during sustained runs
Achieved ~370× laser power build-up gain through alignment and lock optimisation; stable operation demonstrated in the measurement band.
Cardiff University · Ongoing

Optical Calibration System for Gravitational-Wave Detectors

Role

Independently developing this calibration system under the supervision of Prof Hartmut Grote.

System

Independent optical calibration via scattered-light injection, designed as a non-mechanical calibration cross-check for LIGO-like interferometers.

Issue

Force-based calibration methods (e.g., photon and Newtonian calibration) can be limited by modelling uncertainties in test-mass eigenmodes, mass distribution, and deformation effects.

Action
  • Designed independent optical calibration via scattered-light injection without mechanical test-mass actuation
  • Engineered CAD motor mount and cog-wheel scattering surfaces in OpenSCAD; fabricated components via PLA+ 3D printing
  • Programmed Arduino Uno via Arduino IDE to drive a brushless servo motor rotating the scattering cog-wheel and injecting phase-modulated scattered light back into the interferometer
  • Built Python workflows for response extraction in the detectable MHz regime
Planned Extension

Planned integration of an external laser for frequency sweep/scan measurements to obtain interferometer response spectra for calibration; long-term deployment target includes kilometre-scale gravitational-wave facilities.

Preliminary theoretical studies indicate detectable phase modulation in-band; hardware integration and initial test runs are in progress.
Caltech LIGO Laboratory · Summer 2023

Birefringence Fluctuation Measurements in Crystalline Silicon

Role

Independent project under Prof Rana Adhikari and Prof Yuta Michimura; currently being restarted independently at Cardiff with Dr Keiko Kokeyama using hardware upgrades and improved analysis infrastructure.

System

High-sensitivity polarimetry measurement setup for crystalline silicon optical samples.

Issue

Unquantified birefringence fluctuations represented a material-level uncertainty for next-generation detector design.

Action
  • Designed and executed high-sensitivity polarimetry experiment
  • Built noise budget analysis and sensitivity workflows
  • Validated shot-noise-limited behaviour in measurement band
Produced first upper-limit measurements relevant to next-generation detector material studies.
